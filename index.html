Pick the photos to keep

Choose your photos and they are analyzed right on this device, then you swipe through them one by one to decide what to keep. Nothing is uploaded, and the app never talks to an outside server.

Photos in Google Drive show up as a folder once Google Drive for desktop is installed. On Windows, pick your photo folder under the Google Drive drive in File Explorer. On Mac, find it under Google Drive in the Finder sidebar. The first analysis can be slow while photos download, so make the folder available offline first.
